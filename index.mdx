## How Hue works

New to the app? See [App overview](/guides/hue-app).

1. **Instrument** your app with the [TypeScript](/sdks/typescript), [Python](/sdks/python), or [OpenTelemetry](/integrations/opentelemetry) integration.
2. **Observe** traces, findings, and intents in **Traces** and **User Intent**, or let an agent [investigate production](/agents/investigate-production) over the Hue MCP.
3. **Turn traces into cases** with [Cases from traces](/evaluations/case-from-trace).
4. **Evaluate** runs with [eval sets and evaluators](/evaluations/first-evaluation), including [simulated environments](/evaluations/simulations).

See [Concepts](/concepts) for the names Hue uses in the app, SDKs, and MCP.
